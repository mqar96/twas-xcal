## TWAS-XCAL research software

I am the sole scientific author and maintainer of **TWAS-XCAL (twas-xcal)**, software and benchmarks for ancestry-aware calibration in transcriptome-wide association studies (TWAS). The project studies polygenic inflation, transfer of linkage-disequilibrium (LD) corrections across GWAS ancestries, and finite LD-reference-panel uncertainty.

XCAL-t is an approximate inference method. Its empirical results and limitations are documented in the repository. The research includes counted simulation errors, model-based real-genotype comparisons and a descriptive Biobank Japan height application; these provide different kinds of evidence and are distinguished explicitly.
